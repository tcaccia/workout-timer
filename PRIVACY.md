# Privacy Policy

Workout Timer is an offline interval timer for configurable workout plans.

The app does not require an account and does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

Workout plans and saved workouts are stored on the user's device. They are not sent to a server by this app.

The app does not use analytics, advertising SDKs, or third-party tracking.

If this changes in a future version, this policy should be updated before publishing that version.
